ABOUT

Hello, my name is Nguyễn Ngọc Tín. I’m a software engineer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, holding an Engineer in Information Technology degree from Saigon University, majoring in Information Systems. As a recent graduate, I am actively focusing on Backend Development and Cloud Infrastructure.

My primary focus is building RESTful APIs, designing database schemas, and building systems on Cloud platforms. My core tech stack includes Python, Java, TypeScript, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, and AWS. I approach software development as a system problem-solver, believing that tools and technologies are secondary to a strong problem-solving mindset.

I’m a guy with a curious mind. I like to learn something new every day. Whenever I explore new tools, I rely on official documentation and always start from the core fundamentals to build solutions that are solid, effective, and maintainable. Technology is vast and constantly evolving, which keeps me grounded and reminded of how much more there is to discover.

This website serves as my personal knowledge base and digital garden — a space where I keep my study notes, architecture concepts, code snippets, and technical write-ups.

Outside of engineering, I spend my time reading books across various fields, including technology, science, psychology, and literature.
