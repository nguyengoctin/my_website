ABOUT

Hello, my name is Nguyễn Ngọc Tín. I’m a software engineer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. I graduated from Saigon University with an Engineer in Information Technology degree, majoring in Information Systems.

I’m a curious guy. I like to learn something new every day, and lately that curiosity has been pulling me in two directions — building backend systems and figuring out how to make AI actually useful in real products. I spend most of my time writing APIs, designing databases, and tinkering with things like LLM applications, RAG pipelines, and AI agents. My go-to tools are Python, Java, TypeScript, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, and AWS, but I try not to get too attached to any specific stack. Tools come and go; understanding the fundamentals is what sticks.

Whenever I pick up something new, I start from official docs and first principles. No shortcuts, no skipping ahead. I care more about understanding why things work than just knowing how to use them.

This website is my digital garden — a place where I keep study notes, architecture concepts, code snippets, and technical write-ups as I learn.

Outside of engineering, I read across technology, science, psychology, and literature.
